Privacy policy

Aquarium Life Sim — Bozca Games

Effective date: 25 September 2026. This policy describes Aquarium Life Sim (com.aquarium.life.sim). Privacy and support contact: darkbozca@gmail.com. Developer / data controller: Oğuzhan Bozça (Bozca Games).

Your aquarium stays on your device

The game stores aquarium progress, settings, the selected age group, and reward records locally on your device. The game has no account, login, developer server, or cloud save. These local records are not uploaded to Bozca Games. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can permanently erase them. Android app backup is disabled for this release.

Optional advertising

Players who select age 18 or older may choose a rewarded advertisement for 50 AquaCoin. Advertising is disabled for players who select under 18 or prefer not to say. The advertising consent flow and Google Mobile Ads initialization begin only after an eligible player chooses Watch ad. You can play without choosing advertisements. There are no banner or interstitial advertisements, and real-money purchases are disabled in this release.

Information handled by Google

When you use optional advertising, Google AdMob and its consent service may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or other identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics such as performance and crash information. Google uses these data for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Transmission uses encryption. This release does not request the Android advertising ID permission, precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone access. Other SDK identifiers can still be processed. Advertising behavior also depends on your privacy choices and location.

Choices and deletion

You can avoid advertising data collection by not selecting Watch ad. Where Google requires a privacy options entry point, Settings > Privacy choices lets you review or change your advertising choices. Changing a choice does not erase data already processed. Android Settings > Apps > Aquarium Life Sim > Storage > Clear storage deletes local game data. The game has no online account to delete. Contact darkbozca@gmail.com about privacy or support correspondence; do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. Google controls retention and deletion of the data it processes under its policies.

More information

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. If you email support, your email address and the details you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Updated versions of this policy will show a new effective date.

Support records and rights

Support emails are used to respond to and follow up on your request and deleted when no longer needed, except where retention is legally required. Send access, correction or deletion requests to darkbozca@gmail.com. Local game data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Applicable data protection rights remain available.

İsteğe bağlı reklamlar

18 yaş ve üzeri seçeneğini işaretleyen oyuncular, 50 AquaCoin için ödüllü reklam izlemeyi seçebilir. 18 yaş altı veya Belirtmek istemiyorum seçeneğinde reklamlar kapalıdır. Reklamların işleyişi gizlilik tercihlerine ve bulunduğun bölgeye de bağlıdır.
